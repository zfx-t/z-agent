  HTTP-date + cap, status matrix, network-error retry, headers-timeout retry,
  abort during sleep, attempt cap, `policy: false`, callback payloads.

  `vi.getTimerCount() === 0` after each case.

  retry:false / partial merges, httpErrorMessage 500-char truncation and
  body-read-failure tolerance.

  backoff each): openai-responses.test.ts 29, openai-completions.test.ts 22,
  anthropic-messages.test.ts 21 — all assert no `start` event on failed
